1.0 System Architecture & 1-Click Login Personas

The Apex Enterprise ERP is engineered for high-precision multi-location supply chain management. It enforces strict ACID compliance where every physical inventory movement writes an immutable debit/credit transaction to the stock ledger.

Default Credentials: All seeded accounts use the universal password Password123!. You can switch between demo personas instantly from the top navigation bar or the login screen.

2.0 Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) Governance

The ERP features a granular permission engine. Each role is bound to a defined subset of the 25+ permissions seeded in the PostgreSQL database. Separation of duties prevents conflict of interest (e.g., users cannot approve their own purchase requisitions).

How to Manage Users & Review Permission Matrix (/users) ADMIN ONLY
  1. Log in as admin and navigate to Users & Permissions from the sidebar.
  2. User Directory Tab: Search by employee name, corporate email, or department. Review assigned warehouse scopes and status.
  3. Suspend / Reactivate: Click the Suspend button on any active user to revoke immediate API token validation.
  4. Role Assignment: Click + Role to add secondary roles or remove existing role badges.
  5. Permission Matrix Tab: Review the interactive matrix showing exactly which granular gates (e.g., inventory:write, procurement:approve) each role possesses.

Step-by-Step Topology Setup Instructions WAREHOUSE_MANAGER
  1. Navigate to Warehouse Tree (/hierarchy) in the sidebar.
  2. Expand any node to inspect child facilities, warehouses, and storage zones.
  3. Click on any Bin Code (e.g., BIN-A-01-01) in the tree. The right-hand inspector will instantly display:
    • Bin storage type (STORAGE, STAGING, or COLD_STORAGE).
    • Physical on-hand items, batches, and unit quantities currently occupying the bin.
  4. To expand warehouse capacity, click Add Bin, select the target facility, zone, and rack, then specify the unique bin barcode.

4.0 Workflow 2: Product Master Catalog Management

Standardized item records ensure accurate replenishment thresholds, barcoding, and automated low-stock warnings.

Adding and Managing SKUs (/products) INVENTORY_MANAGER
  1. Navigate to Product Master (/products).
  2. Click + New Product to open the item creation dialog.
  3. Fill in required parameters:
    • SKU Code: Unique product identifier (e.g., SKU-BRG-200).
    • Product Name: Descriptive item label (e.g., Precision Ball Bearing 12mm).
    • Category & UoM: Select item group and unit (Pieces, Kilograms, Liters).
    • Inventory Thresholds: Set Min Stock (10), Max Stock (500), and Reorder Level (25).
    • Financial Values: Enter standard Cost Price ($) and Selling Price ($).
  4. Save the record. The system automatically computes stock status badges: NORMAL, LOW STOCK, or OUT OF STOCK.

Phase 1: Submitting Purchase Requisitions (/procurement) DEPARTMENT_MANAGER
  1. Log in as dept_manager and visit Procurement & PO.
  2. Click New Requisition. Select Department, Product, Requested Quantity, and provide justification.
  3. On the draft row, click Submit Approval. This transitions the document into SUBMITTED status and invokes the sequential approval engine.
Phase 2: Reviewing in Approval Inbox (/approvals) FINANCE_APPROVER
  1. Switch persona to fin_approver. Notice the inbox counter badge updates.
  2. Visit Approval Inbox (/approvals).
  3. Inspect the requisition details, justification, and estimated total expenditure.
  4. Choose one of three authorized actions:
    • Approve Step: Advances workflow to final approval and updates underlying PR to APPROVED.
    • Return for Edit: Sends document back to draft for the initiator to revise quantities.
    • Reject: Immediately closes the requisition with mandatory reason comments.
Phase 3: Generating Vendor Purchase Order (/procurement) PROCUREMENT_OFFICER
  1. Switch persona to proc_officer.
  2. Click Create Purchase Order. Choose registered Supplier, Product SKU, contracted unit price, and payment terms.
  3. Save the PO. It is assigned a tracking number (e.g., PO-2026-001) and set to ORDERED.

6.0 Workflow 4: Goods Receiving Dock & Bin Putaway

Receiving at the dock does not immediately increase general sellable balance until items pass quality check and are placed in specific bins.

Dock Receiving & Bin Putaway Procedure (/receiving) WAREHOUSE_STAFF
  1. Log in as wh_staff and open Receiving & Putaway.
  2. Generate GRN (Goods Receipt Note): Click Generate GRN when the delivery truck arrives.
    • Select Supplier, Receiving Warehouse facility, Product, and delivered quantity.
    • Enter physical inspection breakdown: Accepted Qty (e.g., 95) vs Damaged Qty (e.g., 5).
    • Specify the supplier's Batch/Lot number for traceability.
  3. Automated Putaway Task: The system automatically writes the GRN and generates a pending putaway task for the accepted units, targeting the optimal bin.
  4. Confirm Bin Placement: In the Pending Putaway Tasks tab, review the target bin code (e.g., BIN-A-01-02) and click Confirm Putaway to Bin.
  5. ACID Ledger Posting: The system atomics writes a RECEIPT posting to the double-entry stock ledger, crediting available balance in that specific bin.

Executing Inter-Facility Transfers (/transfers) WAREHOUSE_MANAGER & STAFF
  1. Visit Stock Transfers (/transfers).
  2. Click Initiate Transfer. Select Source Warehouse, Destination Warehouse, Product, and requested units.
  3. Dispatching: When freight departs, click Dispatch Stock. Enter carrier name and freight tracking code. Source on-hand balance is immediately debited, and units move to IN_TRANSIT.
  4. Destination Intake: When the truck arrives at the receiving facility, the destination staff clicks Receive Goods, selects the local destination bin, and records actual count.
  5. Discrepancy Ledger: If received units are less than dispatched, the system flags a discrepancy shortage and triggers an audit log.

8.0 Workflow 6: Physical Cycle Counting & Reconciliation

Cycle counting reconciles system balances against physical reality without stopping warehouse operations.

Cycle Count & Variance Reconciliation (/stock-counts) INVENTORY_MANAGER & STAFF
  1. Visit Counts & Variance (/stock-counts).
  2. Click New Cycle Count. Select Target Warehouse and methodology (Cycle Count, Full Inventory, Spot Check).
  3. The system captures an immutable snapshot of current balances across all bins.
  4. Record Physical Counts: Staff walks aisles and records counted physical items. Select reason code if variance is detected (SURPLUS, LOSS, DAMAGE).
  5. Post Adjustments: The Inventory Manager reviews computed variances and clicks Post Adjustments. The system posts atomic ADJUSTMENT_UP or ADJUSTMENT_DOWN ledger records, synchronizing balances.

9.0 Workflow 7: Valuation Reports & Audit Trail

Valuation Intelligence & CSV Export (/reports) FINANCE & AUDIT
  1. Navigate to Reports & Analytics (/reports).
  2. Review the total inventory valuation card, calculated dynamically using standard cost across all warehouses.
  3. Click Export CSV to download a formatted spreadsheet report for accounting records.
Immutable Compliance Audit Trail (/audit) AUDITOR
  1. Navigate to Audit Trail (/audit).
  2. Filter by entity (StockMovement, PurchaseOrder, User).
  3. Click View Diff on any row to open the modal showing side-by-side JSON snapshots of the system state before and after the modification.
